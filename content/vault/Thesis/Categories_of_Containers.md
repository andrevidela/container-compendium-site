## Categories of Containers

Containers and their morphisms form different categorical structures, this section will
show them and also display their proofs. In total we will juggle 3 categories of containers
all with different properties and tradeoffs, $\Cont$ the cartegory of containers and
dependent lenses, $\ContCart$ the category of containers and cartesian morphisms, and
$\cat{Cls}$ the category of closed lenses.
